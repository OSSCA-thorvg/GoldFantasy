Thor and Jormungandr

Thor comes to Midgard for a few nights: by day he lives among the residents, by night he hunts monsters with his hammer. Jormungandr lies in the sea around Midgard and rises in bad weather. Each pair below is the first version on the left and the reworked one on the right.

The serpent rings the whole world and bites its own tail, as in the myth. The top-down check shows the loop, the coils that break the surface and the head; cyan marks a coil at the edge of the map.
