Environment

Trees, mountains and land colours

A resident stands 24 pixels tall at zoom 1. Next to them a grown fir was only 33 pixels, so forests read as lawns. Trees were raised first, then the mountains had to grow to stay above them, and a colour redesign along the way was undone.

Trees

Same cameras, same map. A grown fir went from 33 to 78 pixels at zoom 1.

Mountains

The first attempt stretched each one-tile pyramid up to 225 pixels and read as a field of needles. Now a peak’s height follows how deep it stands in its range: foothills on the edge, a few big summits with shoulders and snow caps deep inside, and no peak over the forest taller than its base is wide.

Land colours

A redesign darkened the trees to a teal close to the ground colour and turned the worlds beyond the gates into pale islands. It was rejected: trees, grass, ground, rocks and peaks went back to the earlier colours, keeping only the drifting water and the gate sprites.
